About Home Cool Point

Delhi NCR's most trusted partner for premium air conditioning and home appliance care since 2015.

Redefining Appliance Care in Delhi NCR

At Home Cool Point, we started with a simple observation: finding a reliable, professional, and honest appliance technician in Delhi NCR was far too difficult. Customers frequently faced duplicate spare parts, hidden charges, delayed arrivals, and zero accountability when repairs failed.

We built our company to change that. By hiring only certified, background-verified specialists and establishing fixed upfront pricing, we've brought transparency and peace of mind back to home services. From split AC gas refills to centralized VRV/VRF planning and commercial maintenance, we handle it all with a premium standard of care.
